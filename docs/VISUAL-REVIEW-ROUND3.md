On 2026-11-23 an independent reviewer examined nine Chromium Canvas and WebGL
stills and the decoded H.264 landscape chapter card. The three thumbnails
were also checked at 320×180 and 160×90.

| Visible criterion | Landscape | Portrait |

| Shared landscape thumbnails | 10/10 | 10/10 |
| **Assessable score** | **65/69 (94.2%)** | **65/65** |

Both views met the 85% static review gate. The portrait score covers the
stage and chapter views supplied. Timing, flash safety, repeatability and
performance are measured separately by the browser and render tests.
[Rubric and browser checks](VISUAL-QA.md)

The revised Mandarin captions preserve the tested phrase breaks. The active
host is identifiable from label brightness and its marker. The 3D pedestals
have clearer edges and the foreground spectrum clears their silhouettes.
All thumbnail variants use the export palette, and the housing headline
identifies its subject. The reviewer found no visible P0 or P1 issue. Faint
diagonal banding remains on close inspection of the decoded landscape card.

Keep Waveform as the default. A 1080p H.264 comparison found that 1200 kbit/s
increased file size without materially reducing the peak measured gradient
step. Fine Bayer dithering stays at the existing 900 kbit/s.
